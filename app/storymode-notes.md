## SIGNAL LOST mechanics

The campaign is intentionally not a quiz skin. Every chapter changes persistent world-state values (`evidence`, `trust`, `pressure`, `integrity`) that influence the final rank. The player is rewarded for building an auditable model under uncertainty rather than simply choosing the most agreeable statement.

A wrong choice is retained as INTEL and still awards a small amount of XP. The campaign can be resumed from local state, and the ending feeds into a real-world reflection prompt.
